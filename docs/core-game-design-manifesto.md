# Project Genesis Core Game Design Manifesto

Version: 1.0

This document defines the long-term vision for Project Genesis. Whenever future gameplay systems are added, they should reinforce this vision rather than drift away from it.

## Game Pillars

Project Genesis combines the best ideas from:

- Cells to Singularity: long-term civilization progression, idle growth, scientific discovery, meaningful evolution, and technology progression.
- No Man's Sky: procedural exploration, planet discovery, star system exploration, resource collection, colonization, and discovery journals.
- Modern Roblox idle games: accessible progression, automation, collection, incremental growth, replayability, and long-term goals.

Project Genesis should feel like:

> I am guiding the evolution of a civilization from survival on Earth to becoming a galactic civilization.

Not:

> I am rushing to reach the Future Age.

## Core Game Loop

```text
Explore
-> Discover
-> Research
-> Gather Resources
-> Build
-> Automate
-> Expand
-> Colonize
-> Evolve Civilization
-> Unlock New Era
-> Repeat
```

Every loop should feel rewarding.

## Player Fantasy

The player begins as a fragile civilization surviving on Earth. Over hundreds of hours they gradually become:

- Planetary Civilization
- Spacefaring Civilization
- Interstellar Civilization
- Galactic Civilization

The journey is the game. The destination is never the only goal.

## Era Progression

Project Genesis has eight primary eras:

1. Survival
2. Ancient
3. Medieval
4. Industrial
5. Modern
6. Space Age
7. Interstellar
8. Galactic

Players should spend meaningful time inside every era. Earlier eras should remain useful forever. Do not make old gameplay obsolete.

## Do Not Reward Rushing

Players should not rush toward Galactic. Encourage:

- Exploration
- Collections
- Research
- Optimization
- Automation
- Achievements
- Mastery
- Rare discoveries
- Historical milestones

Returning to previous eras should remain rewarding.

## Alignment System

The Era system answers: "How advanced is our civilization?"

The Alignment system answers: "What kind of civilization are we becoming?"

These systems are independent.

Canonical alignments:

- Industry: manufacturing, mining, infrastructure, megaprojects.
- Technology: research, science, discovery, innovation.
- Cyber: automation, robotics, artificial intelligence, machine integration.
- Nature: biology, terraforming, living ecosystems, environmental harmony.
- Corporate: trade, economy, commerce, influence, logistics.

Alignment values range from 0 to 100. They do not add up to 100. Players are encouraged to create hybrid civilizations.

## Alignment Influences

Alignment changes through:

- Research
- Buildings
- Automation
- Resource extraction
- Colony focus
- Planet specialization
- Mission choices
- Diplomacy
- Trade
- Exploration

No single decision dramatically changes alignment. Alignment is the result of many choices over time.

## Emergent Civilization Identities

Players never directly choose these. They emerge naturally.

Core identities:

- Industrial Empire
- High-Tech Singularity
- AI Collective
- Gaia Civilization
- Corporate Consortium

Hybrid examples:

- Industrial Technocracy
- Machine Singularity
- Innovation Syndicate
- Interstellar Conglomerate
- Bioengineering Society
- Sustainable Industrial Alliance

These identities evolve over many hours of play.

## Discovery

Discovery is one of the core pillars. Everything discovered should matter:

- Galaxies
- Sectors
- Star Systems
- Planets
- Resources
- Life
- Civilizations
- Colonies
- Anomalies
- Artifacts

Every discovery is permanent. The Discovery Library becomes the player's encyclopedia.

## Colonization

Colonies are persistent. They grow, consume resources, produce resources, expand, require management, and can become major worlds. Colonies are not disposable.

## Resource Philosophy

The Resource Catalog should be grounded in real science. Prefer:

- Periodic Table elements
- Natural compounds
- Minerals
- Alloys
- Manufactured materials
- Biological materials

Reserve speculative materials for very late game. Manufactured resources should never naturally appear on planets. They should be produced by civilizations.

## Production Chains

Resources should evolve:

```text
Iron Ore
-> Iron
-> Steel
-> Industrial Components
-> Spacecraft
```

Old resources should remain important.

## Exploration

The universe should feel effectively infinite:

```text
Milky Way
-> Unlimited procedural sectors
-> Unlimited procedural systems
-> Unlimited procedural planets
```

Later galaxies should use the same procedural engine.

## Automation

Manual gathering exists only early. Players gradually unlock workers, factories, colonies, trade, AI, and automation. The game becomes increasingly strategic rather than click-heavy.

## Economy

Resources should matter. Supply, demand, markets, trade routes, prices, colonies, and factions should connect.

## Mission Design

Missions should emerge from the simulation:

- Scan planets
- Discover civilizations
- Build colonies
- Establish trade routes
- Research technologies
- Solve shortages
- Escort trade convoys

## Design Principles

Every new feature should strengthen one or more pillars:

- Survival
- Exploration
- Science
- Production
- Automation
- Civilization
- Discovery

Do not add systems that exist only to increase complexity.

## Project Genesis Goal

Project Genesis is not simply a procedural planet generator. It is a Civilization Evolution Simulator.

Players should feel like they are writing the history of a civilization over hundreds of hours. Every planet discovered, colony founded, technology researched, civilization encountered, and alignment choice should contribute to a unique civilization story that belongs only to that player.
